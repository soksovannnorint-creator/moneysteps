How we calculate

  • Planned expenses = housing + food + transport + education + debt + other essentials + flexible spending.
  • Available balance = income − planned expenses − planned savings. If this would be below zero we show it as a shortfall, never as spendable money.
  • Spending this month adds up the expenses you log. It’s compared with your monthly income, and nothing is logged automatically.
  • Saved toward goals is the total you’ve recorded across your goals. Nothing is added automatically.

Figures you typed are your own entries; anything marked “estimate” is calculated by the app.
